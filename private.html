01The flight

Take off from the ground. Land where you started.

Earlier jet wings launched from helicopters and ended under a parachute. The Manta Wing stands you up on the pad, lifts you vertically, pitches over and flies on the wing. When the flight is over it brings you back and sets you down on the pad.

Every flight runs at a Talaria site, with our crew on the ground and the flight computer in the loop.

02Progression

Earn your way to full control.

Mode 1

Autonomous

You ride. The wing flies a set route and lands itself, so your first flight is about the feeling of transition and speed.

03Always on

Some protections never switch off.

Expert mode removes the limits on how you fly. It does not remove the systems that bring you home.

Parachutes

A ballistic canopy for the whole wing, and a personal reserve that fires if you separate.

Engine out

Lose a turbine and the other five are re-balanced to recover and land.

Fuel reserve

It turns for the pad while there is still fuel to get there and land.

One-handle release

A single red handle separates you from the wing.

04Access

By application.

The private program opens once crewed flight testing is complete. Flights run at Talaria sites with our crew, and training comes before Expert mode. Private ownership follows certification.
